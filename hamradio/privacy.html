Privacy Policy

Ham Radio Test Prep

Last updated July 11, 2026

Ham Radio Test Prep is a study tool for the FCC Amateur Radio license exams (Technician, General, and Amateur Extra). This policy explains what information the app handles and, more importantly, what it does not.

The short version: Ham Radio Test Prep collects no personal information whatsoever. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no data ever leaves your device.

Information We Collect

None. Ham Radio Test Prep does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. Everything the app creates as you study stays on your device:

  • Your answers, exam scores, flashcard ratings, and study progress are stored locally using Apple’s SwiftData framework.
  • Your track selection and reminder preferences are stored locally using standard iOS settings (UserDefaults).
  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking services are integrated in the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or contact information
  • No amateur radio call sign or license details
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No data shared with, or sold to, any third party

Network Access

The app functions entirely offline. All question pools, lessons, and diagrams are bundled inside the app itself, so no internet connection is used or required to study. The only links that open a network connection are the ones you tap intentionally — this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service — which open in your browser.

Study Reminders (Notifications)

If you choose to enable study reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are handled entirely by iOS and involve no external server or third party. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children’s Privacy

Because Ham Radio Test Prep collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no information from children. The app contains no ads, no in-app messaging, and no external content feeds.

Your Data and Deleting It

All of your study data lives only on your device. You can erase it at any time using Reset All Data in the app’s Settings, or by deleting the app, which permanently removes all associated data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Because the app collects nothing, any changes will relate to clarity rather than new data practices.
